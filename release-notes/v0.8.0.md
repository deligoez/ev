A drawer photo attached in one step, and `ev ui` no longer loses its first keypress in a
terminal that ignores its picture query.

## New

- **`ev photo cut`.** `ev photo cut drawer.jpg --place K4x4-07-A A3=0.1,0.3,0.3,0.1 B4=…`
  puts the whole view on the drawer and a crop on each box in one command, where it took one
  `ev photo add` per box. Every reference is resolved and every crop cut before anything is
  recorded, and all of it is recorded together, so a mistyped code leaves nothing half
  attached. `--note` labels them all; `--place` is optional.

## Fixed

- **The first keypress in `ev ui` could be lost** in a terminal that does not answer the
  picture-protocol query. ratatui-image asked on a thread of its own and, after its timeout,
  left that thread waiting on the input, where it took the next key or appearance report.
  `ev ui` now asks itself and reads the answers with the rest of its input; it starts on half
  blocks and switches to the terminal's picture protocol as soon as the answer arrives, which
  also makes it open faster. Inside tmux the old query stays, since it knows how to pass
  through tmux.

Placement that shows its work: `ev suggest` now ranks the holders and says why, knows when
nothing fits and how much room each box has, and `ev regroup` proposes moves, merges and
bigger boxes.

## New

- **Ranked, explained suggestions.** `ev suggest` scores every holder on its theme, name and
  note and on the names, tags and notes of the things inside it (BM25F with IDF: a part code or
  "LDR" decides more than "sensör"). Each entry shows the words that matched, where they came
  from and how many points each gave, and `considered` says how the score is made. Ties go to
  the lower id, so the same question always gets the same answer. Every holder is still listed.
- **Turkish word forms meet.** `kutuda`, `kitabı`, `fotodirençler` find `kutu`, `kitap`,
  `fotodirenç`; endings are cut only as far as the inventory's own words allow, and a word
  written on its own is never cut. Part codes stay whole (`KY-018`, `HC-SR04`).
- **Knowing when nothing fits.** `coverage` is how much of the description the best holder
  matched; under half, `new_group_likely` says the thing needs a new group.
- **Room.** Each holder carries `room` (yes / little / none / unknown) from its fill, marked
  `stale` when the contents changed after the fill was estimated.
- **`ev suggest --for <thing>`** places something already recorded by its own name, tags and
  note, never into itself.
- **`ev regroup [<drawer>]`** reports things that would fit better in another box, mixed boxes,
  full boxes with a bigger spare box and the cells it would fit in, nearly empty boxes that
  could merge, and unknown or stale fills. It only reports.
- **Sizes.** `--size 1x2x0.5` / `edit size=` records a box's footprint and height in grid units;
  a box tagged `boş kap` with a size is a spare `regroup` can offer.
- **Synonyms.** `ev synonym add "fotosel, ldr, ışık sensörü"` makes a query with one word search
  the others too; `ev synonym list|remove`.
- `ev show` prints a node's size.

## Changed

- The skill's placement procedure: describe the thing with its category words, read the
  reasons, propose a new group when `new_group_likely`, check rules and room, say what was
  weighed, and turn corrections into themes, rules or synonyms; `ev regroup` at the end of a
  drawer's tour.
- The database moves to schema version 9 on first use; older `ev` builds refuse it (exit 6).

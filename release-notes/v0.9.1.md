## Fixed

- **A drawer's grid map was out of view in `ev ui`.** It came after every field and the list of
  contents, and the details pane does not scroll, so a drawer with a photo and twenty boxes
  never showed it. The map now sits right under the drawer's path.

  own; the pane now says how many photos there are, and the photos themselves stay on the panel
  above.

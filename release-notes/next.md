## New

- **A node shows its tasks.** `ev show` (and the details pane of `ev ui`) lists the unfinished
  tasks linked to the node, and those linked to a place that holds it — a task on a drawer
  concerns every box in it — with `via` naming where the link is. Before, a task's places could
  only be read from the task's side.

Everything waiting, in one list. `ev todo` gathers what a home has to do — tasks, moves,
errands, things leaving, labels, things to buy, repairs, use-by dates, lost things — without
copying state that already lives on the records, and `ev ui`'s plan tab becomes Yapılacak,
one collapsible section per kind.

## New

- **`ev todo`.** One list of everything waiting. Moves, errands, disposals, lost things and
  uninventoried places are read where they live and leave the list by their own verbs
  (`done`, `gone`, `back`, `found`), so nothing is kept twice.
- **Labels to print.** Setting or changing a code marks its label as needed; `ev label` lists
  them for the label maker and `ev label <ref>…` marks them printed.
- **Things to buy or make.** `ev need add "Samla 5 L" --qty 2 --for Kiler`, `--make` for a 3D
  print; `ev need list|got|drop`.
- **Broken things.** `ev broken <ref> --note "…"` and `ev fixed <ref>`.
- **Use-by dates.** `ev expires <ref> 2026-07`; due within 60 days or past shows in `todo`.
- **Sales in progress.** `ev sale <ref> --listed --price 500 --where Sahibinden`, then
  `--reserved`; selling it is still `ev gone`.
- **Unclear records.** `todo` lists records whose names are still a guess.
- **A node shows its tasks.** `ev show` and the details pane list the unfinished tasks linked to
  the node, and those on a place that holds it, with `via` naming where the link is.
- **Yapılacak tab in `ev ui`.** Tab 7 shows one section per kind with its count, hides empty
  ones, and points every line at its node; Enter or → opens and closes a section, ← goes up to
  it. The details pane shows a node's label, repair, use-by date, sale and needs.

## Upgrade notes

- **Schema version 7** (`marks`, `needs`), migrated on open; an older `ev` refuses the file
  with exit 6.
- **Existing codes carry no label mark.** Whether their labels exist is not recorded; mark them
  with `ev label <ref>` (printed) or `ev label --needed <ref>`.
- **`ev ui` tab 7** is now called Yapılacak.

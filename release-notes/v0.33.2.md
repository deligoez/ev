A follow-up to v0.33.1: a place that already carried both photo marks still showed both.

## One photo state, also for marks made before

v0.33.1 made `ev photo stale` drop an earlier "still current" mark, but a place marked both ways
before that kept both, and `ev show` printed "photo still current" next to "photo out of date".
`ev show` now shows only the later of the two. It also gives `photo` in its JSON: whether the
place's photos still show it and, when they do not, why (`none`, `changed`, `marked`), when the
newest photo was taken and when its contents last changed, as `ev todo`'s photos list decides
it; the state could only be guessed from the marks before.
